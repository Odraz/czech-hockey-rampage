# 🏒🥅 Czech Hockey Rampage! 🏒🥅

Lace up your skates and sharpen your sticks! Dive into Czech Hockey Rampage, a retro-fueled, side-scrolling beat 'em up where the ice is your battleground and the opposing team is... well, let's just say they're in for a slashing good time!

## To the game! https://odraz.github.io/czech-hockey-rampage/

## The Goal?
Guide two legendary Czech hockey heroes, Jágr and Eliáš, on an epic quest through a hostile hockey stadium. Your mission: to fight your way through relentless waves of Swedish rivals, outmaneuver a surprisingly agile referee, and finally face off against the ultimate frosty fortress – the formidable Swedish Goalie Boss!

## Gameplay Highlights:

Tag Team Takedown: Play as two distinct Czech legends!

Jágr (Player 1): Unleash devastating melee attacks with his trusty hockey stick. Whack-a-Swede has never been so satisfying!

Eliáš (Player 2): Snipe from a distance, firing pucks with pinpoint accuracy. Goooaaal... of destruction!

Golden Axe on Ice: Inspired by the classics, progress through the arena, clearing out enemy waves to unlock the path forward.

Enemy Onslaught: Battle generic Swedish players, a rule-bending referee (who drops a sweet power-up when defeated!), and the imposing Goalie Boss.

Power Up!: Defeat the ref to grab health boosts or temporary damage increases. Time to go beast mode!

Simple Controls, Big Fun: Easy to pick up, whether you're using keyboard or the on-screen mobile controls.

Retro Vibes: Pixelated sprites and a pumping chiptune soundtrack (you'll have to imagine that last part for now, but the sprites are real!) bring the old-school arcade feel to life.

## Why?
Because sometimes, you just need to take hockey rivalries to the next level. And who doesn't love a good beat 'em up with a nationalistic twist? 😉
